Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about campus resource sharing on ResourceX.

Who can register on ResourceX? ▼
Any active student, researcher, or faculty member can create an account directly using their personal email address.
What happens if a borrowed physical item is damaged or not returned? ▼
Every borrow request records start and return dates. If a return is overdue, the borrower receives automatic notifications and their borrowing privileges are paused. Users can also specify a refundable security deposit for high-value lab apparatus. Unresolved issues can be reported to campus moderators.
Can I sell used books or electronics? ▼
Yes! You can list any academic-related textbook, scientific calculator, development board, or drawing kit for sale with a transparent price. The transaction takes place directly and safely in-person on campus.
What types of digital resources are permitted? ▼
You may share personal lecture notes, revision cheat sheets, laboratory guides you have written, past assignment templates with permission, and open-source academic code. Copyrighted pirated textbooks or confidential exam papers are strictly forbidden and will be moderated.
How does the Smart Matching system work? ▼
If an item you need is not currently listed, submit a requirement description (e.g., "Need Raspberry Pi 4 with camera for computer vision project"). ResourceX's matching algorithm compares keywords, department, and categories against incoming listings and notifies you when matches appear.
